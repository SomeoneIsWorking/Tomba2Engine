## What it is

The fifth member of the `FUN_80027A4C` scaled-sprite family. A stunned enemy owns a type-0x20 effect
node whose behavior is `0x8002B7B0` and render function is `0x8002B3A4`. The emitter places four
textured star sprites at the cardinal points of a rotated local ring around that enemy.

## Native producer

`Render::fxSpriteRender` handles `FN_RINGROT`. It reads the node's Euler angles and authored state,
builds the rotation through `MeshQuads::rotmat`, applies the guest's per-column factors through
`MeshQuads::composeScaled`, composes with the native scene camera, projects four local points, and
feeds their shared record list to `spriteRecordsEmit`. It is read-only and contains no GTE taps.

## Root cause and evidence

The initial native port divided the Q12 rotation by 4096 and skipped the guest's immediate
`Math::matColScale` call. `generated/shard_4.c:2498` shows the complete chain: `FUN_80085480`, three
bytes at `0x800A1CD4..D6 << 2`, `FUN_80084520`, then camera composition. The old conversion collapsed
the four centres to less than one pixel; preserving Q12 without the missing scale made the ring 64x
too large. The shared fixed-point composition now reproduces both operations.

`replays/bugs/stun-stars.pad` supplies the previously absent real-game reproduction. The live node is
visible, has a non-null stunned owner, and carries valid record/tpage/CLUT data. On one execution,
native f2799 and live `renderpath psx` f2800 both show the four-star cluster. The native diagnostic
reports `drawn=4/4` and four distinct centres spanning about 28x8 pixels. The regression test is
`tests/test_mesh_quads_math.cpp` (`tomba_mesh_quads_math`).

The preliminary psxport `9f1bb927` repin forced recomp substrate re-emission; the definitive
`692b9b20` pin then received a clean Clang 22.1.8 rebuild. Its bounded SBS run reached f2802 cleanly;
native A and `PURE-ORACLE(interp+softGPU)` B both retain the cluster at f2799. Native reports
`drawn=4/4` with centres spanning 28.38x7.89 pixels. All six final-pin A/B captures and all 61 ring
telemetry lines are byte-identical to the retained 9f run (`scratch/logs/repin_692_stun.log`).
