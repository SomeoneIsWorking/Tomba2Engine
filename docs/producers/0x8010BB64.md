## What it is

The four untextured gouraud quads forming Area 21's early-phase sky. The field backdrop dispatcher
special-cases background state 21 to `FUN_8010BE30`; at the reached `variant == 1 && phase < 4` state,
that controller calls this helper and returns. The Y origin is a signed function of the raw camera pitch,
and the four bands reproduce guest colour words `0x00AC0606`, `0x00EA9898`, and `0x00390000`.

## Native producer

`Render::area21SkyGradientRender` rebuilds the four bands as native `RQ_BACKGROUND` gouraud quads. The
display pass captures the helper's raw pitch input once per logic frame; the presentation re-run uses the
same producer with the pitch interpolated between those captures. The guest body remains installed and
solely owns packet-pool and ordering-table writes, hence the expected `has_native: false` alongside a
nonzero native primitive count.
