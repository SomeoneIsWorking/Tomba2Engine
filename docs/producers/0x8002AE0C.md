## What it is

The small packed-mesh highlight the queue-A object walker adds after an ordinary object mesh or tether.
The node type selects either a fixed scale (`type & 0x40`) or the signed field at node+0x80
(`type & 0x80`). `replays/bugs/bucket-softlock.pad` reaches three highlighted nodes together in the
seaside field.

## Native producer

`Render::objectHighlightRender` rebuilds the controller's rotation, non-uniform scale, adjusted anchor,
field-mode cue and OT bias from persistent guest state, then feeds the existing native owner of the
36-byte record format, `Render::meshQuadRecordsEmit`. It runs from `fieldObjectsRender` only when the
guest's own queue snapshot says the node was submitted and its live jump-table route is the mesh/tether
arm. The installed guest leaf remains the sole owner of guest scratch, GTE, packet and OT writes.
